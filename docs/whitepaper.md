# SPRL Whitepaper

## Vision and mission

A coin belongs to the people who hold it. Today a lookalike with a louder ticker can pull attention away from the original and leave its holders with a choice between watching and starting over. SPRL gives those holders a third option: a vote. The mission is to let a community decide what its coin is called, while the mint, the chart and the crowd stay exactly where they are.

## Roadmap

1. **Phase 1 — Signal.** Public site, live market board, social presence, contract address published.
2. **Phase 2 — Ballot.** Holder-weighted ticker polls with transparent tallies.
3. **Phase 3 — Fire.** Burn mechanism goes live, with a public view of every burn.
4. **Phase 4 — Open doors.** Tooling so other communities can run the same loop for their own coins.
